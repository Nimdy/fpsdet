# CS2 matchmaking (CS2CD) with the dataset authors' labels: detector evaluation

Generated by `fpsdet evaluate` (fpsdet.evaluation/2). Measurement only: nothing here was read by the scorer, and no threshold, role or decision was changed or chosen from it.

## Read this first

- The labels are the CS2CD authors' judgement, not ground truth and not bans. Hand-labelled cheater is a reviewer's call on one match.
- There are two comparison groups, and they differ by construction: hand-reviewed players shared a lobby with a cheater, and unreviewed players did not and were never looked at.
- Every player is one match. fpsdet is built to judge an account over many matches, so most players here are held for too little data. That is the largest effect on every rate.
- The data has server hits, hitgroups, penetration, distance, the view turn before a shot and movement speed. It has no recoil kick, player command, visibility, audio, wire timing, challenges or parties, so those detectors are not observable here.
- The baseline is unreviewed play, screened for whole lobbies of outliers. One unlabelled cheater left in it raises the bar for everyone.
- This is one public dataset of CS2 matchmaking. It says nothing direct about another game, mode or season.
- Every rate is among players a detector could run on. A rate below 20 players is not shown; below 100 in either main group, a detector is descriptive only. Intervals are two-sided 95% Wilson intervals. No p-values.
- No status here is "calibrated". The strongest is "measured": on this population, with these labels.

## What the labels mean

| Label | Role | Who | What it does not mean |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| hand-labelled cheater | positive | A player the dataset authors labelled as cheating, after reviewing a match that contained a VAC-banned player. | A ban. The labels are the authors' judgement. |
| hand-reviewed, not labelled | comparison | A player in a reviewed match whom the authors did not label as cheating. They shared a lobby with a cheater. | Proven clean. It is a reviewer's call, but a direct one. |
| unreviewed match | comparison | A player in a match with no VAC-banned player. Not reviewed: the authors' spot check of 50 such matches found 97.2% of players showing no cheating. | That anyone looked at this player. A few cheaters are likely here. |

## Limitations

- Unit: one player in one match. Each match is pseudonymised on its own, so a player cannot be followed across matches.
- Match level: the same as the player level: every player is one match.
- Observation-level counts (per weapon or metric) have no intervals: one player's weapons are not independent.
- A rate here describes this population, labelled this way. It is not the rate on another game, league or season, and it is not a probability that any one player cheated.
