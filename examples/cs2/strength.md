# Evidence strength: cs2cd-2026-10-04

Generated by `fpsdet evaluate strength` (fpsdet.strength/1). Offline research: the scorer never reads it, and nothing here is a threshold, a weight, or a probability that any player cheated. The direct measurement is the evaluation it was estimated from; this is a separate research layer.

## Read this first

- **hand-labelled cheater** (positive): A player the dataset authors labelled as cheating, after reviewing a match that contained a VAC-banned player. Not: A ban. The labels are the authors' judgement.
- **hand-reviewed, not labelled** (comparison): A player in a reviewed match whom the authors did not label as cheating. They shared a lobby with a cheater. Not: Proven clean. It is a reviewer's call, but a direct one.
- The labels are the CS2CD authors' judgement, not ground truth and not bans. Hand-labelled cheater is a reviewer's call on one match.
- There are two comparison groups, and they differ by construction: hand-reviewed players shared a lobby with a cheater, and unreviewed players did not and were never looked at.
- Every player is one match. fpsdet is built to judge an account over many matches, so most players here are held for too little data. That is the largest effect on every rate.
- The data has server hits, hitgroups, penetration, distance, the view turn before a shot and movement speed. It has no recoil kick, player command, visibility, audio, wire timing, challenges or parties, so those detectors are not observable here.
- The baseline is unreviewed play, screened for whole lobbies of outliers. One unlabelled cheater left in it raises the bar for everyone.
- This is one public dataset of CS2 matchmaking. It says nothing direct about another game, mode or season.
- The number below is a **label-conditioned evidence ratio**: how much more often a detector fired for hand-labelled cheater players than for hand-reviewed, not labelled players, among players it could run on. It is a likelihood ratio in form only. With these labels it is not one, and it never becomes a weight.
- Each detector is estimated alone, on "fired at least once". Estimates are never multiplied together: findings on one player share cohorts, matches and telemetry.

## The estimator

- Each group's firing rate: Beta(0.5, 0.5) prior (Jeffreys), posterior Beta(fires + ½, eligible − fires + ½), posterior mean.
- The ratio: positive posterior mean over comparison posterior mean. Finite even with no comparison fires.
- Its credible set: from each rate's central 0.9747 interval, so it holds the ratio with at least 95% posterior probability.
- No estimate below 20 eligible players in either group of the development half; measured from 100.
- Stability: **replicated** (the evaluation half fell inside the development prediction in both groups, and its own set excludes 1), **tentative** (consistent, but too small or not excluding 1), **unstable** (outside the prediction), **unsupported** (no development estimate, or it does not exclude 1). One split never validates anything.
