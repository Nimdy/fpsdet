## Read this first

- **RGL cheating-ban labelled** (positive): RGL banned the account, and the reason mentions cheating but not assisting, distributing or developing cheats. Each had at least 20 team matches on logs.tf before the ban, and its 20 latest were fetched. Not: That the player cheated in every scored match, or in any particular one. A ban is a league decision, sometimes mirroring another league's.
- **never-banned comparison** (comparison): RGL never banned the account for anything. It played in the same lobbies as labelled accounts, with at least 8 matches in the fetched logs. Not: That the player never cheated. A few unlabelled cheaters are likely among them.
- The labels are RGL's, not ground truth. RGL cheating-ban labelled means RGL banned the account and the reason mentions cheating. It does not mean the player cheated in any particular scored match.
- The comparison is never-banned, not proven honest. A few unlabelled cheaters are likely in it.
- The data is per-match totals: shots, hits, sniper headshots and kills per minute. There is no timing, position, view angle or movement, so most detectors are not observable here.
- Shot times are spread evenly through each match by the converter, so no timing-based detector could mean anything on this data.
- Exposure is unequal. Most never-banned players have a few matches and labelled accounts have up to 20, so compare at equal evidence (the evidence-amount table).
- This is one season of competitive TF2. It says nothing direct about another game, league or season.
- The number below is a **label-conditioned evidence ratio**: how much more often a detector fired for RGL cheating-ban labelled players than for never-banned comparison players, among players it could run on. It is a likelihood ratio in form only. With these labels it is not one, and it never becomes a weight.
- Each detector is estimated alone, on "fired at least once". Estimates are never multiplied together: findings on one player share cohorts, matches and telemetry.

## The estimator

- Each group's firing rate: Beta(0.5, 0.5) prior (Jeffreys), posterior Beta(fires + ½, eligible − fires + ½), posterior mean.
- The ratio: positive posterior mean over comparison posterior mean. Finite even with no comparison fires.
- Its credible set: from each rate's central 0.9747 interval, so it holds the ratio with at least 95% posterior probability.
- No estimate below 20 eligible players in either group of the development half; measured from 100.
- Stability: **replicated** (the evaluation half fell inside the development prediction in both groups, and its own set excludes 1), **tentative** (consistent, but too small or not excluding 1), **unstable** (outside the prediction), **unsupported** (no development estimate, or it does not exclude 1). One split never validates anything.
