How a line becomes a case.

Four outcomes: review, watch, clean, or held (insufficient_data). automated_action is always none. recommended_action is human_review, monitor, or none. A person still decides. This does not end cheating.

The decision

Gear rules can open a review on their own. Combat rates need a thick cohort of humans first. A thin cohort does not flag. The build is reported as untrained.

Condition Decision

Past the rank only, a single ceiling hit, a history jump, or two supporting familieswatch
A record from another integrity system, however many agreewatch, at most

Nothing was comparableheld

A sustained gear break is speed, fire interval, blatant or learned recoil, a same-tick recoil mirror, a metronome on a legal cycle, sustained tracking of a hidden mover, sustained aim on a private replay, aim that matches the wire snapshot ahead of the picture, or aim noise that drops only while the server says this client could not have known.

The four checks, drawn

Each picture is a planted pair from the desk. One opens a case. The other shares the headline number and stays clean. The exact bars are in the cards under it.

Gear, from the first match

Speed.

Only samples on the ground with displacement_cause of none are scored. The cap is expected_max_ground_speed_mps on that sample, else the weight curve in the profile. Over cap means faster than the cap times 1.08. The finding needs a run of at least 25 of those samples, with gaps no larger than 400 ms. A blast, a vehicle, a parachute, an ability, an unknown cause, a cause the profile has not listed, and any airborne sample are excluded. A shorter run is written down as a possible glitch. It does not open a case. The planted desk shows a 10 kg kit at 7.1 m/s, cap 5.6, beside the same sprint tagged as a blast.

Fire interval.

Gaps under that weapon's own minimum, past one tick of slack, and enough of them. This runs even when the aim sample is still too small to score. Stamps that all land on the server tick, and a gun the server itself fired, stay quiet.

Recoil.

Counted once the spray index is at least 3, and the cause is not an innocence tag. The floor is expected_min_recoil_pitch_deg on the shot, else the profile row for that build. A run of 10 shots under a quarter of the floor is a review. A compensated gun has a lower floor, so the same pitch can be legal. No floor and no thick cohort: untrained, not flagged. The steadiest legal player on a build is not a review.

Same-tick mirror.

The camera can still kick. Send the kick the server applied and the signed player command on that same tick. Review when at least 16 shots correlate at −0.90 or below, and that correlation is at least 0.25 more negative than the same series shifted one shot later. A person pulls on a later shot. In a game with a fixed spray pattern, practiced players pull it on time, so the average kick and command at each spray index are removed first and only the part of the kick that changes between sprays is tested. With too few sprays the check waits, unless the profile says every kick is random.

Metronome.

Fire interval catches gaps under the legal line. A macro that fires on the legal line, with no motor noise, does not. Review at 40 or more gaps, a mean on the legal side of the line, and a standard deviation of 1.0 ms or under.

A fact this client was not given

Hidden tracking.

hidden_track_ms is time, on that shot, since the previous shot, that the aim stayed on an enemy this client could neither see nor hear. Omit it, or send 0, and nothing happens. Audio-labeled shots, and shots within a second of last seeing or hearing that enemy, do not count: following footsteps or a body that just ducked out of sight is human. Review when at least 8 shots are above 0 and the sum is at least 1200 ms. One long sample is not enough. A visibility query that marks a visible enemy as hidden manufactures the case. That is an emitter bug.

Private replay.

private_track_ms uses the same bar. The body is someone else's real movement, on another heading, where this client's sight and audio queries both fail. There is no invisible flag. A short crossing stays clean. A body this client could see, labeled private, manufactures the case. The desk draws that route. The score reads only private_track_ms.

A planned challenge sends challenge_id and challenge_track_ms instead, bound to a plan the server made from its secret, and the same bar holds per challenge: 8 moments and 1200 ms. Version 2 counts a moment only when challenge_vision_state and challenge_audio_state both say absent. One seen or heard moment voids the challenge, and so does a contradiction. An unchecked channel abstains. The bar counts time, not intent: aim held still on an angle, with the body resting behind it, can reach it. Challenge reviews are experimental and not production-qualified. What the dry run found.

The picture is late.

wire_error_deg is the error to the snapshot the server just sent. picture_error_deg is the error to the position the official client draws, one interp_delay_ms earlier. Omit any of the three and that shot does not count. A shot counts as wire-led when the delay has started, the picture is at least 0.20° farther than the wire, and the wire error is at most 0.35 of the picture error. That gap is a separation floor, not an accuracy ceiling. The same bar applies: 8 wire-led shots and 1200 ms of summed delay. Standing still does nothing. One shot does nothing. The same hits on the picture stay clean. A server that scores the wrong timeline manufactures the case. There is no new profile knob. This runs even when the aim sample is still too small to score.

Unknowable smoothness.

information_state is visible, audio, or unknowable. Audio is knowable. Review when both sides have at least 12 samples, the knowable median is at least 0.05°, and the unknowable median is at or under 0.35 of that. A player who is smooth the whole time stays clean. A wrong unknowable label manufactures the case.

After the decision

The same leftover.

Subtract the server kick and the previous kick. What remains is averaged per spray index and compared across accounts on the same build, at least 24 shared spray indices, correlation at or above 0.85. A match is a watch, not a review. If one account is already a review, the other moves up the scan. If neither is a review, both are watches.

A teammate, before a voice.

Only after someone in the party is already a review for a hidden mover. Four or more swings on that same still-hidden enemy, in the same match, under 350 ms, are a watch. A lag long enough to have been a voice stays clean.

Reports.

They change queue order. They do not change the decision, and they are not in the seal. A brigade can make you look today. It cannot convict anyone.

The seal.

SHA-256 of the player, the game, the decision, and the reasons. Edit a reason and the hash changes. It identifies what a reviewer saw. It is not a ban. Beside it, the evidence packet digest binds the findings, the decision, and digests of the code, the settings, the baseline and the events that produced them. Evidence.

Other systems' records.

fpsdet score --external carries records from client anti-cheat, platforms or leagues as given: provider, class and confidence. One can move a clean case to watch. None, and no number of them together, makes a review. A record signed with a key the operator registered is verified: known to come from that key, not known to be true.

Human ceilings.

One number per player, leave-one-out, in that rank and on that weapon. The cohort is thick at 30 players after the subject is removed. The bound is a one-sided 95% Wilson lower bound, widened when the rate swings between matches more than chance. Past every measured human is review-grade; the same number on two guns counts once. Past the rank only is a watch. A hot ten-shot game has a wide bound and does not clear a maximum.

The precise bars, every default, are docs/scoring.md in the repository. The planted answers are on the desk.
