No profile in this repo is a studio export. Do not ban from a copied community table. A server that simulates the player has already applied a speed cap and a recoil for the current stance, perks, and attachments. Send those numbers. The curves in profiles/ are the fallback when a sample has no expected value, and they are how the idea is unit-tested. This does not end cheating.

Fields a server for these games could fill

Field Why it exists
weapon_id, mod_setRecoil and aim per build. Unknown builds stay untrained.
loadout_weight_kgThe kit they are wearing, after the server's own weight math.
expected_max_ground_speed_mpsThe cap for this stance, this weight, this perk, this second.
speed_mps, on_groundWhat the body did, and whether it was a sprint rather than a jump.

Extra numbers are a row in extra_metrics plus the field on the event. ADS time, stamina, sway, ergonomics, and time-to-kill fit that slot. The line itself is on Wire a game.

WARDOGS

Public gear guides describe weight as a movement rule. They list five equipment classes with about these move penalties: lightest 0–10 kg with no penalty, light 10–17 kg about −5%, medium 17–27 kg about −13%, heavy 27–40 kg and super-heavy about −20%. Those penalties are in profiles/wardogs.json. Absolute meters per second are not. A wrong constant would flag legal players. The server's expected_max_ground_speed_mps is the number that scores.

The same guides describe an adrenaline pen that lowers the equipment weight class. If the server works that way and you score against the raw kit weight, a legal pen looks like a speedhack. Send the cap after the pen, the stance, and the class math.

Tag anything the game has that moves a body (parachute, explosion, ragdoll, vehicle, ladder) for the whole throw, not one sample. On the planted desk, weight-cheat is about 10 kg of kit sprinting at 7.1 m/s, on the ground, cause none. blasted is the same speed with the cause set, and it stays clean. adrenaline is the pen: the curve would flag the weight, and the server cap does not.

Escape from Tarkov

From public play, the same shape as WARDOGS, only harsher. Weight and inertia appear to change acceleration and top speed. The weapon, the attachments, the ammo, and ergonomics appear to change recoil and ADS. A server that applies those effects should send its post-effect speed cap and its recoil floor for the assembled gun. A profile of guessed ergo numbers will false-flag.

Grenades, ragdolls, stimulus or pain effects that launch the body, and admin or disconnect corrections are innocence causes. If part of the felt recoil is presented only on the client, score the view delta the server replicated during the spray. A client-only camera shake is not evidence, because a cheat will not play it.

Call of Duty

If the build is the weapon plus an attachment list (Gunsmith), the build key is that list, not the weapon class. If recoil differs by barrel, muzzle, underbarrel, and optic, send the floor the server computed for that build.

If movement caps change by stance, tac-sprint, slide, dive, swim, and perk or specialist speed stay legal when expected_max_ground_speed_mps is the cap of the stance they are in. A profile that only knows walk speed will call tac-sprint a cheat. Tag vehicles, redeploys, and explosions as innocence causes. A killstreak that moves the player or fires for them should be its own weapon_id or an ability cause, so a VTOL does not enter an infantry accuracy cohort. Use the matchmaking bracket of that playlist as the rank band.

Battlefield

Vehicles are likely the biggest source of false positives. A player inside a vehicle, on a wing, under a parachute, or flung by destruction is not an infantry sprint. Tag vehicle, parachute, explosion, or knockback for the whole interval, including the exit if the exit launches them.

Do not put vehicle-seat accuracy into the infantry rifle cohort. Send a distinct weapon_class for vehicle weapons so the human ceiling is other vehicle gunners. Tag traversal gadgets as ability or launch. On very large maps, one distance baseline means little. map_id is on the event for your own splits. The reference cohort keys distance by weapon and rank.

What training means

Training is the cohort file fpsdet baseline writes. It is the distribution of humans on each band, weapon, and build, from the lake you dumped. A new gun, a new mod combination, or a new weight class with no curve stays untrained until min_cohort_players humans have enough samples. Flagging a build you have never measured is how legal players get removed.

The optional brief does not do this training. It explains a case after the numbers exist. A sustained break of the server's own speed cap does not need a model. The bars are on Scoring. The files are on Source.
